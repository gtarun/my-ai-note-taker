## What the app does

AI Notes records audio you choose to record, stores it on your device, and — when
you explicitly ask it to — turns that audio into a transcript and summary using
either on-device processing or an AI provider you configure with your own API
key.

this app to record anyone without the permission the law requires.

**Your content.** You are responsible for the recordings you make and what you do
with the transcripts and summaries the app produces.

**Your provider accounts.** When you configure a third-party AI provider, you use
your own account and API key with that provider, and you are bound by that
provider's terms and billing. Any charges they levy are between you and them.

## Accuracy

AI-generated transcripts and summaries are frequently inaccurate. They may
misattribute statements, omit important points, or state things that were never
said. Do not rely on them as a verbatim record, and review anything important
against the underlying audio. The app is not suitable as the sole record for
legal, medical, financial, or other consequential decisions.

## Acceptable use

Do not use AI Notes to:

- record anyone without the consent the law requires;
